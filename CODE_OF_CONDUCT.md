# Code of conduct

Chatnip follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, assume good intent, keep criticism about the work and not the person, and make it easy for anyone to take part regardless of background or experience.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting on this repository, marked as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.
